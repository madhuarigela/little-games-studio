# Privacy policy: Star Sprout Sprint

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

Star Sprout Sprint is an offline game. The app does not request an internet connection, create an account, show advertisements, use analytics, or collect, transmit, or share personal information. Game progress and preferences (including the best score, collected seeds, and sound setting) are stored only on the device using Android app storage.

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. The app is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If the app's behavior changes, this policy and the Play Store disclosures must be updated before release. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact information on the app's Google Play listing.

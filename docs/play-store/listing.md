# Google Play listing draft

## App name
Star Sprout Sprint

## Short description (draft)
Dash through floating gardens, dodge playful hazards, and gather seeds.

## Full description (draft)
A tiny courier. A sky full of garden paths. How far can you run?

Guide an original little sprout through bright, floating biomes. Switch lanes, leap over roots, slide beneath branches, and gather seeds along the way. Unlock new looks and discover fresh color palettes as your best distance grows.

• Easy swipe controls: change lanes, jump, and slide.
• Quick runs with a pause button and personal bests.
• Collect seeds and unlock original outfits.
• Choose from three original garden worlds.
• Play offline; no account required.

Star Sprout Sprint is an original arcade runner. Optional ads or purchases may be added in a later release; if present, they will be disclosed in the Play listing and in-app before use.

## Graphics specs
- Store icon: 512 × 512 PNG, sRGB, under Play Console's current file-size limit; original sprout silhouette and distinct color field; no promotional text or misleading badges.
- Feature graphic: 1024 × 500 PNG/JPEG, original sky-garden panorama and title-safe central area.
- Phone screenshots: 4–8 portrait captures showing home, controls, active run, pause, results, world selection, outfit room, settings/privacy. Capture real app UI; don't mock features not in build.
- Tablet screenshots only after tablet layout is verified. Avoid competitor comparisons, ratings claims, and ad-free claims unless true.

## Closed testing plan
- Create internal test first; upload signed AAB; verify install and billing test accounts.
- Closed test: recruit at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days when required by the developer account's Play Console track rules. Ask testers to install, play several sessions, report device/OS and issues, and remain opted in through the full window.
- Track crash-free sessions, ANRs, tutorial completion, run length, control issues, and battery/heat. Do not collect personal data without a disclosed need/consent.
- Promote only after all P0/P1 issues closed and privacy/ads/purchases/content-rating declarations match the shipped build.
